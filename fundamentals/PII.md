\# Personally Identifiable Information (PII)



\*\*Personally Identifiable Information (PII)\*\* is information that can identify a specific person, either by itself or when combined with other information.



\## Examples of PII



Common examples include:



\* Full name

\* Home address

\* Email address

\* Phone number

\* Passport number

\* Driver's license number

\* Social Security or national identification number

\* Bank account information



\## Why is PII important in cybersecurity?



If PII is exposed, attackers may use it for:



\* Identity theft

\* Fraud

\* Phishing

\* Social engineering

\* Account takeover



Organizations should protect PII using appropriate security controls such as:



\* Access controls

\* Encryption

\* Secure storage

\* Data minimization

\* Proper deletion



\## Example



An email address by itself may identify a person, while a combination such as:



> Full name + address + date of birth



can provide much more identifying information.



\## Key takeaway



> \*\*PII is information that can identify or help identify a person and therefore needs appropriate protection.\*\*
