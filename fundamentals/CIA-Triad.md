\# CIA Triad



The CIA Triad is a fundamental model used in cybersecurity. It represents three important security principles:



\* \*\*Confidentiality\*\*

\* \*\*Integrity\*\*

\* \*\*Availability\*\*



\## 1. Confidentiality



Confidentiality means that information should only be accessible to authorized people or systems.



\### Examples



\* Password-protected accounts

\* Encryption

\* Access controls

\* Private medical or financial information



\*\*Question to remember:\*\*

Who is allowed to see the information?



\---



\## 2. Integrity



Integrity means keeping information accurate, complete, and protected from unauthorized modification.



\### Examples



\* Preventing unauthorized changes to a database

\* Using hashes to detect file changes

\* Restricting who can modify important records



\*\*Question to remember:\*\*

Has the information been changed without authorization?



\---



\## 3. Availability



Availability means that authorized users should be able to access systems and information when they need them.



\### Examples



\* Backups

\* Redundant servers

\* Protection against denial-of-service attacks

\* Disaster recovery



\*\*Question to remember:\*\*

Can I access the system or information when I need it?



\---



\## Easy way to remember



\*\*Confidentiality → Who can see it?\*\*



\*\*Integrity → Is it correct and unchanged?\*\*



\*\*Availability → Can I access it when needed?\*\*
